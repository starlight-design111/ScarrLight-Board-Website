Problem Statement

Tower defense games are mainly the same idea where you are on a top down view, and have to buy and place towers on a given part of the map. This can be (most likely) anywhere on the map that Isn't a path for the enemy to reach and destroy your base or hurt you in any way. STS plans to innovate on the way that tower defense is played, allowing for more skilled play and more critical thinking when in game.

Innovation Claim

STS is a system where you can place towers, but each tower has 1-3 weapon slots, one filled when you buy and place it, and another for when you get a new weapon. 3 slot towers have an exponential cost per tower base. All towers can have the weapons removed and the player can use them for manual defense alongside the towers, and both the weapons and tower bases can be upgraded separately. Weapons you grab have limited ammo and have to be replenished at the tower you picked It up from. The weapons themselves have elements that work with each other to increase damage delt, stun enemies, DOT enemies (damage over time), and more. Elements can be used as a weapon as well (oil for fire, water for ice/chill).

Project Description & Scope

This project is about changing the way that you normally play tower defense games by making a new gameplay mechanic that changes up the gameplay loop. The idea is to keep the player more engaged in defending rather than only watching the towers do all the work. A first person view would work in this case. The project will be done solo so I will perform all the roles of coding, modeling, and playtesting as well as a few other minor details. The Main software that will be used will be Unity. The PM system I will use to manage and schedule work time will be monday.com

Technical Expertise & Skills

  • The field that i plan to innovate in is Game design related to how tower defense gameplay loop occurs, and changing the system up to make a new gameplay loop.
  • Classes needed - Classes related to C# (Unity)
  • Technical skills needed – C# skills (Unity) Modeling (blender or Maya Autodesk).

Target Market

This is mainly for either Game Designers looking for a new type of tower defense design or casual gamers that are looking to find a new type of tower defense experience. The demographic is going to be focused on casual players as that is where the most people are in the gaming market. It also aligns with a tower defense game.
