Student Innovation Project (SIP)

STS (Swapper Tower System) • Active First Person Tower Defense

Redefining the traditional top-down tower defense loop by putting players on the ground in first person with detachable, combinable turret weapons and elemental synergies.

Project Visuals & 3D Asset Pipeline

Blender • Hard-Surface Modeling

Modular Tower Bases & Turret Heads

Hard-surface 3D models developed in Blender showcasing scaling L1, L2, and L3 multi slot tower bases and interchangeable turret heads designed to support socket and swapper mechanics.

Problem Statement & Innovation Claim

Core Innovation • First Person Gameplay

Breaking the Passive TD Loop

Traditional tower defense games rely on passive top-down placement where players simply watch towers work. STS keeps players actively engaged in first person defense requiring critical thinking, resource management, and hands-on combat alongside automated defenses.

Modular Mechanics • Weapon Detachment

1–3 Slot Towers & Manual Wielding

Tower bases feature 1 to 3 weapon slots (with exponential scaling costs for 3 slot bases). Players can detach any mounted weapon to wield it manually managing limited ammo that must be replenished directly at its parent tower while upgrading bases and weapons independently.

Elemental Synergies & Combat Systems

Combat Mechanics • Status Effects

Elemental Combos & Utility

Weapons carry elemental properties that interact dynamically to amplify damage, stun targets, and inflict Damage Over Time (DOT). Utility elements such as pairing oil with fire or water with ice/chill reward tactical loadouts and cross tower combinations.

Target Audience • Market Focus

Casual Gamers & Game Designers

Designed to appeal to casual tower defense players looking for a fresh, hands on experience, as well as game designers exploring modular weapon and turret architectures.

Project Scope & Solo Development Pipeline

  • 🛠️ Engine & Programming: Built solo in Unity using C# for modular socket logic, weapon detachment/ammo states, elemental interactions, and first person player controls.
  • 🎨 3D Asset Pipeline: Custom 3D tower bases and modular weapon models created in Blender and Autodesk Maya.
  • 📋 Project Management: Solo milestone tracking, sprint scheduling, and playtesting workflows managed through monday.com.
