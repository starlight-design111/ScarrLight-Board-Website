Student Innovation Project (SIP)

STS: Swapper Tower System

A first-person tower defense game featuring modular mechanics, weapon detachment, and dynamic tactical management.

Modular Tower Architecture

Engineered 1-to-3 slot modular tower bases and socket configurations in Blender, paired with Unity C# backend scripting for real-time weapon swapping and tactical synergy.

Development & Scheduling

Managed via monday.com milestones, combining solo programming, 3D asset modeling, playtesting, and iterative design refinement.
