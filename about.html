About Me

Game Designer & Level Architect

Professional Background & Focus

I am a game design and level architecture student dedicated to building engaging, player-driven mechanics and robust modular systems. My academic and project work focuses on bridging the gap between immersive 3D/2D art pipelines, clean programming logic, and thoughtful level design.

Art & Modeling: Blender, Autodesk Maya, Aseprite, and Substance 3D Painter for hard-surface assets, weapons, and character sprite animation states.

Documentation & Web: Modular HTML/CSS architectures, technical wikis, and agile sprint tracking via monday.com.
